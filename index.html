About Us

Breaking Stigma, Restoring Dignity

Who We Are

Queens of Change Foundation is a grassroots, women-led non-governmental organization dedicated to transforming the lives of underprivileged women and girls across India. We believe menstrual health is a fundamental right, not a source of shame or a barrier to success.

Our work starts directly within rural and urban marginalized communities. We provide free sanitary pads, offer evidence-based health awareness education, and design local vocational workshops to pave the path toward self-reliance.

See Our Programs

Dignity & Respect

Every woman and adolescent girl deserves absolute dignity. We build supportive spaces where periods are normal and respected.

Community-Led

Our solutions are designed, implemented, and refined by local community voices who intimately understand the daily barriers.

Empowerment

We foster lasting independence by providing concrete vocational resources, tailoring mentorship, and health guidance.

Our Programs

Making A Tangible Difference

Our multidimensional approach creates supportive ecosystems, ensuring economic and health barriers never restrict opportunity.

Sanitary Pad Distribution

Providing free, dermatologically safe sanitary pads to underprivileged students and young women. By reducing financial strains, we prevent girls from dropping out of essential schooling during their cycles.

Sponsor Distributions

Health Education Workshops

Dismantling decades of silence. We hold community-led seminars on reproductive health, menstrual biology, and safe self-care habits for young mothers, adolescents, and local leaders.

Support Workshops

Vocational Skilling

Unlocking financial freedom. We conduct local training in stitching, computer literacy, and micro-entrepreneurship, transforming community women into confident breadwinners and local organizers.
